For reference — Braxton's twelve

From his own handout, in the Anthony Braxton Papers at the Library of Congress. The signs are described here in words; the handout is on the Library's site.

  1. Long sound — a straight line
  2. Accented long sound — a narrow wedge that runs on as a line
  3. Trills — a line of tall waves
  4. Staccato line formings — a dashed line
  5. Intervallic formings — a line that jumps up and down in square steps
  6. Multiphonics — three small five-sided shapes
  7. Short attacks — three small open triangles
  8. Angular attacks — a chain of sharp Z shapes
  9. Legato formings — a long smooth wave
  10. Diatonic formings — a smooth wave with small strokes across it
  11. Gradient formings — a smooth wave with arrowheads along it
  12. Sub-identity formings — a small square, then a line

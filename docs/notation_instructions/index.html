Approaching
for bass flute, bass clarinet, percussion, viola, cello and electronics
Justin Yang, 2026

Performance Instructions

The score for Approaching is in a computer-animated digital format. It is accessed and played like an online video game through a web browser.

Instrumentation

Bass flute, bass clarinet, percussion, viola, cello, electronics.

Percussion (one player): bongos, shime-daiko or snare drum, high toms, bass drum, wood blocks, China cymbal, suspended cymbal, spring coil, crotales, glockenspiel, xylophone, marimba. The cymbals and the crotales are also bowed.

Electronics: each player has one close microphone; the percussionist's single microphone covers the whole set-up. The electronics are heard in stereo over loudspeakers.

The full score is in C. Parts will be transposed.
